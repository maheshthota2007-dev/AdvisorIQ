Apple Inc. is a technology company that designs and sells smartphones,
personal computers, tablets, wearables and related services.

Apple generates revenue from products such as the iPhone, Mac and iPad,
as well as services including the App Store, cloud services and digital
subscriptions.

Technology companies can face risks from changing consumer demand,
competition, supply-chain disruptions, regulatory changes and rapid
changes in technology.

Investors should consider company financial performance, valuation,
business risks, industry conditions and broader market conditions when
researching a company.

Historical stock performance does not guarantee future returns.
