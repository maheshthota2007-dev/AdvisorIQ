Market investments involve risk and prices can change rapidly.

Stock prices can be affected by interest rates, inflation, economic
conditions, company earnings, investor expectations and geopolitical
events.

Diversification can reduce exposure to the risks associated with a
single company or asset, but diversification cannot eliminate all
investment risk.

Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future performance.
Investors should evaluate their own objectives, risk tolerance and
investment time horizon before making investment decisions.
